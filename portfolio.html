01 Marketplace · Live

drivingtestmatch

Learner drivers find each other and coordinate a test swap.

Learners post the test they hold and the one they want, and the platform surfaces compatible matches. Chat-first by design — contact details stay protected until both sides agree, listings expire automatically after the test date, and the DVSA still handles the actual booking. Free to browse and post; a one-off £25 unlocks messaging when you find your match.

02 Exam preparation

SQE Academy

FLK2 preparation for the paper most candidates fail.

Built specifically for SQE1 FLK2 resitters. Candidates answer questions with a confidence rating before submitting, then get a worked explanation of why the right answer is right — plus exam technique. Full 180-question mock papers run on a server clock in halves, and an AI tutor bounded to the question being reviewed answers follow-ups without wandering off-syllabus.

  • Confidence-rated answering
  • Worked explanations + exam technique
  • Timed 180-question mock papers
  • SM-2 spaced repetition scheduling
  • Subject mastery & mock readiness dashboard
  • Question-bounded AI tutor
