BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few good questions.

Is this just an AI receptionist?

A receptionist is one part of the workflow. The Lead Engine brings enquiry handling, qualification, follow-up and pipeline visibility into one agreed setup.

Will it work with the tools I already use?

Tell us your website, CRM and calendar providers. We check what can be connected and explain any limits or extra costs before implementation. We don’t promise support for every system.

Does AI make decisions without my team?

We agree what the system can answer, when it should hand over and which actions need approval. Complex requests should reach a person. The sample on this page illustrates the workflow, rather than a production AI conversation.

What happens in the free demo?

We discuss where enquiries arrive, what happens when you miss one and what a useful next step looks like. Then we show a relevant workflow and discuss whether a pilot is a fit.

Are results guaranteed?

No. The calculator uses your assumptions and the demo is illustrative. A pilot should establish a baseline and measure actual enquiry and booking outcomes.

How is customer information handled?

Before connecting live customer data, we agree the information required, access, retention and providers involved. For this demo request, KWBA stores the details you submit to respond to your enquiry. More detail is shown beside the form.
